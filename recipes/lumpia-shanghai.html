Lumpia Shanghai

Description

Lumpia Shanghai is a popular Filipino spring roll that’s traditionally filled with ground pork, onions, garlic and/or vegetables and shallow-fried in a skillet, giving it a light, crisp exterior.

Ingredients

  • Ground pork
  • Carrot
  • Onion, scallions, and garlic
  • Egg
  • Lumpia wrappers
  • Dipping sauce

Steps

  1. Make the filling. Start by mixing ground pork with onion, scallions, lots of garlic, grated carrots, kosher salt, and black pepper.
  2. Fill the lumpia wrappers. Separate the thawed lumpia wrappers from one another. Working with one at a time, lay the wrapper on the work surface with one corner pointed towards your body. Add a bit of the meat filling to one side of the wrapper and form into a 5-inch thin log.
  3. Roll the lumpia. Fold the corner closest to you tightly over the filling, then fold the sides over and crease the edges. Tightly roll the lumpia into a long, thin egg roll and use a dab of the egg white to seal the wrapper shut. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling until you have used everything up.
  4. Fry. Cook the lumpia by shallow-frying in a skillet over medium to medium-high heat until the wrapper is deep golden-brown and crisp and the center is cooked through completely.
