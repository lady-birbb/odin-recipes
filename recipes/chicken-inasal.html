Chicken Inasal

Description

Chicken Inasal is a grilled chicken dish from the Western Visayas region, particularly Bacolod City and Iloilo. The word “inasal” comes from the Hiligaynon term for chargrilled or roasted.

Ingredients

  • Lemongrass
  • Ginger
  • Achiote
  • Brown sugar
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Chicken thighs

Steps

  1. Make the achiato oil. Gently warm the olive oil and annatto powder over medium heat, then let it cool completely.
  2. Marinate the chicken. Whisk the marinade ingredients in a large bowl. Add the chicken thighs, toss to coat, and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or up to overnight.
  3. Grill the chicken. Grill the chicken thighs over direct, medium-high heat, lightly brushing on some of the annatto oil, until cooked through (when the thickest part of the chicken registers at least 165°F on an instant-read thermometer).
